GTasks ("GTasks", "the App", "we", "us", or "our") is a free, open-source desktop application that provides a desktop interface for Google Tasks.

This Privacy Policy explains what information GTasks accesses, how that information is used, and how it is stored.

1. Information GTasks Accesses

When you connect your Google account to GTasks, the App requests permission to access your Google Tasks data.

Depending on the features you use, GTasks may access:

Google Tasks lists
Task titles
Task notes/details
Task due dates
Task completion status
Task completion dates
Other task information necessary to display and manage your Google Tasks

GTasks does not request your Google Account password.

Authentication is handled through Google's OAuth authorization system.

2. How We Use Your Information

GTasks uses Google Tasks data solely to provide the functionality of the application, including:

Displaying your Google Tasks lists and tasks
Creating, editing, and deleting tasks
Creating, renaming, and deleting task lists
Marking tasks as completed or incomplete
Displaying task due dates and other task information
Keeping the desktop interface synchronized with your Google Tasks account

GTasks does not use Google Tasks data for advertising, profiling, selling data, or unrelated analytics.

Our use of Google user data is limited to providing the functionality users explicitly request through the App, consistent with Google's API Services User Data Policy.

3. Data Storage

GTasks is designed as a desktop application.

Google Tasks data retrieved by GTasks is used to populate the application's interface. GTasks does not operate a server that receives or stores your Google Tasks data.

OAuth authentication tokens are stored locally on your computer so that you do not need to authorize the application every time it starts.

GTasks does not intentionally upload your Google Tasks data to a GTasks-owned server.

4. Data Sharing

GTasks does not sell, rent, or share your Google Tasks data with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

GTasks only communicates with Google services when necessary to authenticate your account and retrieve or modify your Google Tasks data.

Google's own handling of your information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and applicable Google terms.

5. Google API Services

GTasks uses Google APIs, including the Google Tasks API.

Your use of Google services through GTasks remains subject to Google's applicable terms and policies.

GTasks' access to Google user data is limited to the permissions necessary for the App's functionality. Google requires applications using its OAuth infrastructure to accurately disclose their data use and request only the permissions necessary for their functionality.

6. Data Security

GTasks takes reasonable measures to protect authentication information and other locally stored application data.

However, no software or computer system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of the computer on which GTasks is installed.

7. Disconnecting Your Google Account

You may stop GTasks from accessing your Google account by removing the application's authorization from your Google Account settings.

You may also remove GTasks' locally stored authentication information from your computer.

Removing authorization prevents GTasks from continuing to access your Google Tasks account until you authorize it again.

8. Children's Privacy

GTasks is not specifically directed toward children under the age required by applicable law to provide consent to online services.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when GTasks' functionality or data practices change.

If material changes are made, the updated policy will be published at the same location with a new effective date.
